Frequently Asked Questions

  • What does a Koala look like?

    Koalas have soft gray fur and white fur underneath.

  • Are Koalas endangered?

    Koalas are classified as vulnerable.

  • How large do Koalas get?

    Koalas weigh about 30 pounds and grow about 2 feet tall.

  • What do Koalas eat?

    Koalas eat eucalyptus leaves and bark.

  • How long do Koalas live?

    Koalas can live up to 17 years.
