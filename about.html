Help Save the Koalas

There is currently no legislation that can fully protect koalas and their habitat.

Below are some ways you can help:

  • Donate to the Koala Foundation
  • Join the Koala Army
  • Write local representatives
  • Post information on social media
  • Adopt a Koala
  • Conduct a fundraiser

What You Can Do

  • Plant Koala food trees
  • Drive carefully at night
  • Keep pets inside at night
  • Provide safe exits from swimming pools
