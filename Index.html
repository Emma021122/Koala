Welcome to the Koala Bear National Information Center!

In this site you can learn about Koala bears, adopt a Koala, give to our foundation, sign up for our newsletter, or schedule a visit.

The Koala Bear National Information Center (KBNIC) is dedicated to the conservation and management of wild koalas and their habitat.

Koalas are disappearing because of habitat loss and destruction of eucalyptus trees.

Koalas are actually marsupials, not bears.
